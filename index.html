How it works

From claim to consensus,
in the open.

One pipeline turns scattered evidence into a price that people can trust, challenge and act on.

File

Anyone files a claim about impact, good or bad, with evidence in any format. Duplicates are caught automatically.

Evaluate

Independent evaluators submit a low, most-likely and high value. Estimates stay sealed so nobody can copy the crowd.

Converge

After five or more evaluations and a one-week cooling period, the extremes are trimmed and the rest form the consensus.

Challenge

Any claim or evaluation can be disputed. Disputes go to Independent Tribunal and the whole history stays public.

Settle

Forecasts are checked against measured outcomes when their window closes, and accurate evaluators build reputation.

Why BaseX

Trust through visible,
challengeable work.

Impact reporting today is mostly self-assessment and PR. BaseX is built so that no single party, including us, decides what something is worth.

Permissionless

There are no gatekeepers and no in-house assessors to train. Anyone can file a claim and anyone can evaluate it, so it scales with the crowd rather than with headcount.

Sealed estimates

Evaluations are committed before others are revealed. Herding and anchoring are designed out, so every estimate is independent.

Robust consensus

The highest and lowest estimates are trimmed and the rest are weighted by confidence. One bad actor can't move the price.

Harms count too

Negative value is first-class. It gives you a full ledger of assets and liabilities instead of a highlight reel, which is the opposite of greenwashing.

Everything challengeable

Every claim, evaluation and dispute is append-only and public. Nothing is deleted quietly, and disagreement is published, not hidden.

Past and future

BaseX prices value that has already happened and value still to come. Forecasts settle against real outcomes, so early-stage moonshots get a price before any market will pay for them.

Human consensus, AI scale

A data flywheel for the value of everything.

Every consensus is a labelled, challengeable data point about what something is worth to the world. That is exactly the ground truth AI needs and doesn't have.

  • AI structures the input: messy reports, photos and spreadsheets become clean claims.
  • AI suggests estimates and people price, challenge and correct them.
  • Human consensus is the baseline: it steers the models and scores every evaluator, human or AI.
  • The more it's used, the sharper it gets: better reputation means better consensus and better data.
Claim structuring is in progress. AI-suggested estimates are designed but not yet built.
